Our service

What we can do for you

From everyday prescriptions to accredited treatments and hard‑to‑find items — if we don’t have it, we’ll work to get it.

Medicine management

We are able to facilitate:

  • Prescription medicines
  • Repeat prescriptions
  • Free courtesy reminders for repeats
  • Emergency supply of prescription medication
  • Compliance blister packaging / Medico Pak
  • Disposing safely of medication, including sharps
  • Medicines reconciliation and synchronisation
  • ‘Yellow Card’ provision

Health services & advice

We’re accredited to provide treatment for:

  • Emergency contraception (ECP)
  • Motion sickness
  • Erectile dysfunction (Viagra / Vedafil)
  • Urinary tract infections (Trimethoprim)
  • Blood pressure monitoring

Accredited services can change. Please call (03) 319 5035 to check availability before making a special trip.

A little extra

  • Complimentary gift wrapping of in‑store purchases
  • Overnight supply of non‑stocked items — we can usually get it by the next day
  • Comprehensive advice on sourcing products and services we can’t immediately access
