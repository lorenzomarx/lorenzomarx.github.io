Before you send

This form opens your own email programme and sends the message to the pharmacy. Email is not a secure channel for health information.

If you’d rather not send medical details by email, please phone us on (03) 319 5035 instead — we’re happy to take your repeat over the phone.

Please allow one working day. We’ll contact you if there’s a delay or if we need to check anything with your prescriber.

Urgent or run out?

Don’t use this form. Call us on (03) 319 5035 during opening hours, or 027 569 4174 for after hours emergencies. For a medical emergency dial 111.
