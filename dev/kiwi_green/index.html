What we do

Seven ways to bring your place back to life.

Every job gets the same slow, careful attention. No rush in, rush out.

Exterior house washing

Eco-friendly soft wash that lifts dirt, moss and mould without blasting your paint. Do it yearly and your weatherboards will thank you.

Driveway cleaning

Concrete, asphalt, brick or garden paths. Our surface cleaner strips grime without scarring the surface.

Gutter cleaning

Blocked gutters lead to roof leaks and foundation damage. We clear them before winter does its worst.

Deck cleaning

Remove algae, mould and slippery build-up, and protect the timber from rot and pricey repairs.

Fence cleaning

A gentle soft wash restores the look, boosts curb appeal and helps your fence last longer.

Solar panel cleaning

Soft brushes, no harsh chemicals, no scratches. Keep every panel pulling in maximum energy.

Window and skylight cleaning

Your home looks newer and brighter in one visit. No streaks, no missed spots, no risky ladder work for you.

Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Njord.

From the Dutch lowlands to Auckland's weatherboards.

Originally from the Netherlands, Njord spent parts of his childhood in Christchurch and Wellington before heading back to Europe. In 2019 he returned to New Zealand for good, building a life around the outdoors, hard work, and helping people.

After a few different jobs he landed on a simple truth: there is something deeply satisfying about turning a tired, weathered property into one that looks its absolute best. That became Kiwi Green Cleaners.

Every job, from a house wash to a gutter clean, gets the same care. No rushing, and always protecting the surfaces being cleaned.

"I treat every home as if it were my own. If I'm not happy with the result, the job isn't finished."
